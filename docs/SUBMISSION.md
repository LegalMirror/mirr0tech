# ETHGlobal Tokyo 2026 submission text

**Project:** mirr0tech · **Tagline:** compile a legal agreement into on-chain policy; every refusal names its sentence.

**Description.** A tokenized fund's transfer-agent agreement says who may hold the token and what onboarding they must clear. On chain that becomes a hand-kept allowlist that cannot tell "not permitted" from "not yet checked", and nothing proves which clause refused a wallet. mirr0tech compiles the agreement itself. Upload the document: a deliberation generates its policy, every rule quoting the sentence it came from, with a verdict and a confidence per claim. Add the World ID constraint the agreement asks for: the credential and the actions it gates go into the policy hash. Deploy: the agreement gets its own oracle, token, Uniswap v4 hook and pool. Prove it from the terminal: facts alone are refused with the sentence, a World ID document proof admits, the investor adds liquidity and swaps through the hook, a stranger is refused with the same sentence and no transaction. A signed payment webhook settles USD into shares under the policy, or holds it with the sentence. Every decision is in the audit with its clause and tx hash.

**How it's made.** Node gateway (`/v1/agreements*`, OpenAPI 3.1 at `/openapi.json`, Swagger UI at `/docs`) and a CLI (`scripts/mirr0.js`) over Solidity compiled at runtime. **Noolog** runs the generation as a deliberation (extractor + critic, model `nsed:deep`): per-claim verdicts and a confidence ship with every policy; an in-process mock serves the same routes offline. The compiler turns the AST into DNF bitmasks with three-valued facts, proves them equivalent to the JS interpreter over every assignment, and hashes source + AST + config into `policyHash`; generated Solidity holds constants only, one evaluator (`PolicyEval.sol`) serves every policy. **World ID** is the KYC fact: the issuer picks the credential per agreement (Passport/NFC document by default), the gateway verifies with `/v4/verify` and a server-signed `rp_context`, rejects the wrong credential or a proof bound to another wallet, binds the nullifier to one wallet, and attests `identityVerified` with expiry. **Uniswap v4**: `MirrorPolicyHook` at a mined address decides on `beforeAddLiquidity` / `beforeRemoveLiquidity` / `beforeSwap`; a transient handshake makes it the token's only door (`NoPolicyDoor` for a hookless pool); one pool per agreement on the canonical `PoolManager`. **Curvegrid MultiBaas** registers every contract under a policy-hash version and indexes the events behind the audit; the operator key can move from a file into a MultiBaas Cloud Wallet (Azure Key Vault HSM) through a platform setting. A Stripe-signed webhook attests `depositConfirmed` and lets the policy decide the mint. Next.js dashboard with a clause highlighter from quote to DNF to bytes to contract.

**Prize tracks.**

- **World (IDKit).** The issuer chooses the credential the agreement's KYC sentence needs and that choice is locked in the policy hash the token carries; a document proof (`issuer_schema_id` 9303) attests `identityVerified`, the fact the token and the hook require. Server-side verification only: `rp_context` signed on the gateway, `/v4/verify`, the wrong credential is `WRONG_CREDENTIAL`, a reused human is `HUMAN_ALREADY_BOUND`. A verified proof is attested on Sepolia; live-app proofs and mock proofs run the same code path (`docs/WORLD_ID.md`).
- **Uniswap v4.** A permissioned token under a permissionless pool: the hook is the token's only door, address mined for its permission bits, admission on liquidity and swaps, and every revert carries `LegalClauseViolation(clauseId, policyHash)` so the front end renders the sentence. One pool per agreement is initialized on the canonical Sepolia `PoolManager` at deploy time. Liquidity and a swap through the hook, and the stranger's refusal, are recorded on Sepolia.
- **Curvegrid (MultiBaas).** 13 contracts registered on the Sepolia deployment under policy-hash versions, 10 linked and indexed under the dev plan; `GET /v1/stack/events` serves the events the audit reads. Key custody is a platform setting: `PUT /v1/settings/signing` registers an Azure Key Vault key with MultiBaas, hands the operator roles and gas to the Cloud Wallet, and the gateway signs from it thereafter. The HSM path is proven on anvil with a stand-in vault; the dev MultiBaas deployment has no Cloud Wallet yet, so no HSM-signed transaction is on Sepolia.

**Sepolia vs anvil.** On Sepolia: the base stack and one agreement (`agr_1b8a5c438bb1`) through the whole flow from the CLI, including the payment webhook (125.50 shares released, one payment held); MultiBaas indexing. On anvil only: the HSM-signed transaction; `test/chain/flow.test.js` runs the whole sequence there. Also in the repo, out of the demo flow: a credit venue (Wildcat `IRoleProvider` + 1inch Aqua/SwapVM buyback) from the same compiler, deployed on Sepolia against the canonical Aqua.
