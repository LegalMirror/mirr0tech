 The legal paper you signed says things like 
 - "you must be an approved investor,"
 - "you can't sell for 90 days,"
 - "everyone together can only cash out 10% a day." 
 
 
 Right now that paper is a PDF sitting in a lawyer's folder, so if the token went onto a normal crypto exchange, none of those rules would follow it — which is why the lawyers say no, and why these tokens can't be traded anywhere. 


Implement this as DeFi (hooks, 1inch aqua based order books etc etc)

 Two nice things fall out: 

 1. for the first time you can see what those rules cost, because if people will only pay 96 cents for something worth a dollar, that 4 cents is the price of the lockups and the waiting
 2. AND, we can protect the people providing liquidity from getting picked off at the moment the fund publishes its new official price, because the document tells us exactly when that happens.
