# Master Loan Agreement (illustrative sample)

**This is a synthetic document written for this repository's tests and demonstration. It is not
Wildcat's published template, not any real borrower's agreement, and not legal advice.** It exists
so the compiler has a source document whose clauses can be quoted verbatim and hashed.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Borrower" means the market maker operating the Market. "Lender" means a person who has been
admitted under Clause 2 and holds Market Tokens. "Market" means the undercollateralized credit
market deployed by the Borrower.

1.2 "Screening" means the checks described in Clause 3, performed by or on behalf of the Borrower.

## 2. Lender eligibility

2.1 No person may deposit into the Market unless that person has executed this Agreement and the
Borrower has confirmed that execution.

2.2 The Borrower shall admit a Lender only where the Lender has completed customer due diligence,
including identification and verification of the Lender and, where the Lender is an entity, of its
beneficial owners.

2.3 The Borrower shall not admit any person who is resident in, or organised under the laws of, a
jurisdiction in which the offering of participations in the Market is prohibited.

2.4 Participation in the Market is limited to persons who qualify as professional or accredited
counterparties under the laws applicable to them.

2.5 The Borrower shall not admit, and shall not permit any dealing by, any person who appears on a
sanctions list maintained by a relevant authority, and shall apply blocks in accordance with
Sanctions Laws.

## 3. Ongoing screening

3.1 Screening is not a one-time condition. The Borrower shall re-screen each Lender at intervals of
not more than thirty (30) days, and a Lender whose screening has lapsed shall be treated as not
having satisfied Clause 2 until screening is renewed.

3.2 Where an adverse result arises between scheduled screenings, the Borrower shall act upon it
immediately and without waiting for the next scheduled screening.

## 4. Deposits

4.1 A Lender admitted under Clause 2 whose screening is current may deposit into the Market up to
the capacity allocated to that Lender by the Borrower.

4.2 No deposit shall be accepted where it would cause the Lender's participation to exceed the
capacity allocated to that Lender.

## 5. Withdrawals and repayment

5.1 A Lender may request withdrawal of principal and accrued interest in accordance with the
withdrawal cycle of the Market.

5.2 No payment shall be made to a Lender in respect of whom the conditions of Clause 2 have ceased
to be satisfied at the time the payment is to be made.

5.3 A Lender may not withdraw before the expiry of the minimum commitment period stated in the
Market's terms.

## 6. Transfers

6.1 Market Tokens may not be transferred except to a person who has themselves been admitted under
Clause 2 and whose screening is current at the time of transfer.
