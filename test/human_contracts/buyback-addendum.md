# Addendum 1 to the Master Loan Agreement — Standing Buyback

**Illustrative addendum for a fictional borrower.** This clause is not part of the Wildcat template Master Loan Agreement. It is the sentence a borrower adds so that a standing buyback can be compiled into a venue. Not legal advice.

## A1) Standing Buyback

A1.1 The Borrower will purchase Market Tokens from any Known Lender at a price of not less than 0.96 units of the Digital Asset To Be Loaned per Market Token, up to an aggregate of 1,000,000 Market Tokens.

A1.2 The offer in A1.1 remains open until 2026-12-31, unless earlier revoked by the Borrower by notice via the Communication Platform.

A1.3 The Borrower may fulfil the offer through a venue of its choosing, provided that the venue admits as counterparty only a Wallet Address holding a valid Deposit Credential, and provided that no funds of the Borrower are committed to the venue before a purchase is executed.

A1.4 Nothing in this Addendum obliges any Lender to sell, and nothing in it varies the Withdrawal terms of the Agreement.
