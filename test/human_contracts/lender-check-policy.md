# Demo MM Ltd — Lender Check Policy

**Illustrative document for a fictional borrower.** The Wildcat template Master Loan Agreement delegates lender admission to a Lender Check Process that the Borrower defines and enforces through a Role Provider. This policy is that definition for Demo MM Ltd. It is not legal advice and describes no real firm.

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This policy sets out the Lender Check Process that Demo MM Ltd (the Borrower) applies before granting a Deposit Credential for any Market it deploys under the Master Loan Agreement.

1.2 A Deposit Credential is granted only by the Borrower's Role Provider and only where every check in Section 2 has been completed with a satisfactory result.

## 2. Checks

2.1 Agreement. The Lender has countersigned the Master Loan Agreement and Term Sheet for the Market from the Specified Wallet Address, and the Borrower has recorded that countersignature.

2.2 Due diligence. The Lender has completed customer due diligence, including verification of the Lender's identity and, where the Lender is an entity, verification of its beneficial owners and of the authority of the person acting for it.

2.3 Anti-money laundering. The Lender has provided the information the Borrower considers reasonably necessary to conduct anti-money laundering and know your customer checks, and those checks have been completed.

2.4 Solvency. The Lender has confirmed that it is not insolvent and is not subject to any bankruptcy or insolvency proceedings.

2.5 Sanctions. The Lender's Specified Wallet Address is not present on the Chainalysis Sanctions Screening Oracle. The Oracle is the definitive source for this check and the Borrower does not substitute its own judgement for it, save as permitted by Section 13 of the Agreement.

## 3. Validity and re-screening

3.1 A satisfactory Lender Check Process result is valid for thirty (30) days from the date the checks were completed.

3.2 A Lender whose result has expired is treated as not having completed the Lender Check Process until the checks are repeated, and shall not be granted or retain a Deposit Credential in the meantime.

3.3 Where an adverse result arises between scheduled screenings, the Borrower shall act on it immediately by withdrawing the affected result, and shall not wait for the next scheduled screening.

## 4. Payments and transfers

4.1 Before any Withdrawal is honoured, the Borrower confirms that the Lender's Lender Check Process result is current at the time of payment.

4.2 Market Tokens may be transferred only to a Wallet Address that holds a valid Deposit Credential, consistent with the Token Transferability level of the Market.

## 5. Records

5.1 The Borrower records, for every decision to grant, withhold, withdraw or restore a Deposit Credential, the check that determined it and the time of the decision.
