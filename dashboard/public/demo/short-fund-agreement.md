# Tokenized Fund Share Agreement (short demo)

This agreement is a fictional demo. It describes no real fund, issuer or asset.

This Tokenized Fund Share Agreement is made as of September 1, 2026 ("Effective Date") between Mirror Treasury Fund Ltd. ("Issuer") and Mirror Transfer Agent LLC ("Transfer Agent").

## 1. Shares
The Issuer issues shares of the Mirror Treasury Fund as tokens on a public blockchain. Each share is recorded by the Transfer Agent.

## 2. Eligible investors
Shares may be issued to or held by an investor only after the investor has passed know-your-customer and anti-money-laundering checks and is not listed on any sanctions list.

## 3. Identity verification
An investor must verify their identity with a government-issued identity document before shares are issued to, transferred to or redeemed by that investor.

## 4. Subscriptions
The Transfer Agent shall issue shares to an investor only after the Issuer has accepted the investor's subscription and the investor's payment has been received by the Issuer's custodian.

## 5. Redemptions
The Transfer Agent shall redeem shares only when the Issuer has authorized the redemption and the investor holds enough shares to cover it.

## 6. Transfers
Shares may be transferred only to an investor who meets the conditions of Sections 2 and 3. Any other transfer is void.

## 7. Price and fees
The net asset value is USD 1.00 per share. The subscription fee is 25 basis points and the redemption fee is 25 basis points.

## 8. Supply cap
The total number of outstanding shares shall not exceed 1000000 shares.
